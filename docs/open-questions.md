# Open questions

Every question this site leaves open, grouped by section, with what is already known, how it could be
settled and what that takes. The ids (`OQ.H`, `OQ.C`, `OQ.P`, `OQ.F`, `OQ.S`) are stable, so pages link
straight to a row; answered questions move to [Closed questions](#closed-questions) with their answer.
The one thing to know first: several checks below write to a keyboard, and a few can wipe or wedge one,
so read the "Needs" column before you start.

!!! danger "Writes can brick or wedge a board"
    Items marked **donor** write to a keyboard or take it apart. Try them only on a board you can
    afford to lose, with a full snapshot taken first. Read [Recovery](recovery.md) and the
    [never-send list](troubleshooting.md#the-never-send-list) before any write.

!!! note "At a glance"
    - 122 open questions: product and hardware 39, connectivity 17, wire protocol 27, firmware and storage 25, host software 14.
    - 25 closed questions, with their answers, at the [end of the page](#closed-questions).
    - 30 of the open questions can be settled with read-only checks on a retail keyboard.
    - Results are most useful with the firmware of both halves (`fe/1002`), the host OS, the date and the raw bytes.

## How to use this page

Each row gives the question and what is known (with its evidence tag), how it could be settled, what
that needs, and the page the question belongs to. "Needs" uses four kinds:

| Needs | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **keyboard (read-only)** | a retail keyboard, modules or dongle, with reads, observation or normal use only; nothing stored changes |
| **donor** | a board that may be written to, wedged or opened: writes, risky probes, teardown or chip reads |
| **running NayaFlow** | the vendor app running (with or without a keyboard), to watch its interface, logs or USB traffic |
| **documents** | installers, registers, archives or other public records; no hardware |

A keyboard check that needs a second computer (a Linux machine, a Mac, a Bluetooth sniffer) says so.
Report a result with the firmware of **both** halves (`fe/1002`), the module firmware if it matters
(`de/1008`), the host OS, the date, and the raw bytes sent and received. Byte strings follow the
[byte convention](protocol/transport.md#byte-convention).
